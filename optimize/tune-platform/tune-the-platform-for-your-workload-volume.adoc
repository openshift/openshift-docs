= Tune the platform for your expected workload volume
:context: tune-the-platform-for-your-workload-volume

toc::[]

[role="_abstract"]
Tune the platform itself, rather than individual services, so that Serving, the Queue Proxy sidecar, and the net-kourier ingress controller are sized for the workload volume you expect.

{ServerlessProductName} components have different resource requirements and scaling behaviors depending on how much traffic your cluster serves.

Start by configuring Serving for your workload size, then adjust the Queue Proxy and net-kourier settings if your traffic profile requires it.
