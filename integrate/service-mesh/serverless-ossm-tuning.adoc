After you integrate {SMProductShortName} 2.x with {ServerlessProductName}, you can adjust the integration to suit your cluster.

Enabling {SMProductShortName} with mutual Transport Layer Security (mTLS) prevents Prometheus from scraping Knative metrics, the {ServerlessOperatorName} generates Istio network policies that you might need to replace with your own, and the Knative `net-istio` ingress controller can consume more memory than necessary on large clusters.
