= Adapt platform components to your cluster
include::_attributes/common-attributes.adoc[]
:context: adapt-platform-components-to-your-cluster

toc::[]

[role="_abstract"]
{ServerlessProductName} ships its control-plane components with defaults that suit most clusters. When your cluster has its own rules for resource requests, probe timings, scheduling, or proxy authentication, adapt those components rather than working around them.

The {ServerlessOperatorName} reconciles the deployments it manages, so edits made directly to a `Deployment` object are reverted. Apply your changes through the `KnativeServing` and `KnativeEventing` custom resources (CRs) instead, so that they survive reconciliation and upgrades.
