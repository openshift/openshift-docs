= Configure HA for Argo Rollouts
:context: enabling-ha-support-for-argo-rollouts

toc::[]

[role="_abstract"]
Argo Rollouts supports high availability (HA) through the `RolloutManager` custom resource (CR). When HA is enabled, the Operator sets the controller pod count to 2 using the `.spec.ha` field and activates leader election for active-passive operation.

[id="prerequisites_{context}"]
== Prerequisites
* You are logged in to the {OCP} cluster as an administrator.
* You installed xref:../install/installing-openshift-gitops.adoc#installing-openshift-gitops[{gitops-title}] on your {OCP} cluster.
* You installed xref:../extend/using-argo-rollouts-for-progressive-deployment-delivery.adoc#gitops-creating-rolloutmanager-custom-resource_using-argo-rollouts-for-progressive-deployment-delivery[Argo Rollouts] on your {OCP} cluster.
