= Configure audit logging and distributed tracing

[role="_abstract"]
Configure compliance audit logging for {ols-official} to produce a durable, reconstructable record of AI actions. Audit logging supports regulatory requirements such as the EU AI Act by capturing who asked what, what the AI proposed, who approved it, and what the system executed. Audit logging is enabled by default on both {ols-long} and agentic {ols-long}.
