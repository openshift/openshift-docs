= Evaluate data privacy and compliance posture

[role="_abstract"]
Before adopting {ols-official}, review how your data is handled, what telemetry and transcripts are collected, and how system health monitoring works to ensure compliance with your organization's privacy requirements.

{ols-official} communicates directly with your configured large language model (LLM) provider endpoint without routing data through {red-hat}-managed cloud infrastructure. Telemetry data collection is optional and can be disabled. {red-hat} does not use customer data to train or fine-tune AI models.
