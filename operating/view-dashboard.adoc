= Viewing the dashboard
include::modules/common-attributes.adoc[]
:context: view-dashboard

toc::[]

[role="_abstract"]
The {product-title} ({product-title-short}) Dashboard provides quick access to the data you need. It has navigation shortcuts and actionable widgets that are easy to filter and customize so that you can focus on the data that matters most to you. You can view information about levels of risk in your environment, compliance status, policy violations, and common vulnerabilities and exposures (CVEs) in images.

[NOTE]
====
When you open the {product-title-short} portal for the first time, the Dashboard might be empty. After you deploy Sensor in at least one cluster, the Dashboard reflects the status of your environment.
====
