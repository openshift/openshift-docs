= Diagnose why a serverless workload is not working
:context: diagnose-a-serverless-workload

toc::[]

[role="_abstract"]
When a Knative service does not respond, returns errors, or never becomes ready, work outward from the workload itself: check what the service reports, then the pod, the revision, and finally the ingress and route that carry traffic to it. Most failures are visible in one of these layers.

Start by inspecting the state of the failing workload. If the cause is not apparent, or if the behavior looks like a defect in {ServerlessProductName} rather than a problem with your configuration, collect diagnostic data and open a support case with Red{nbsp}Hat.

Inspect the state of a failing workload:: Check terminal output, pod and revision status, and Ingress and route status to locate the layer that is failing.

Collect diagnostics and get help from Red Hat:: Search the Red{nbsp}Hat Knowledgebase, gather must-gather data for {ServerlessProductName}, and submit a support case.
