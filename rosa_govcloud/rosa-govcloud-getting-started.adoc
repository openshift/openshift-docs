= Getting started with {product-title} in AWS GovCloud
include::_attributes/attributes-openshift-dedicated.adoc[]
:context: rosa-govcloud-getting-started

toc::[]

[role="_abstract"]
Sign up for a Red{nbsp}Hat FedRAMP account to access {product-title} in AWS GovCloud. FedRAMP-compliant {product-title} clusters enable federal agencies and their supporting contractors to run containerized workloads in authorized government cloud environments.

[NOTE]
====
This service is available to the following organizations:

* Federal and government agencies
* Commercial organizations that support or are bidding on a government contract
* Federal Information Security Modernization Act (FISMA) research and development universities that support or are bidding on a government contract
====
