= Decide whether serverless fits your workloads
include::_attributes/common-attributes.adoc[]
:context: decide-whether-serverless-fits-your-workloads

toc::[]

[role="_abstract"]
When choosing how to run event-driven or bursty workloads, understanding what {ServerlessProductName} provides and where it works best helps you evaluate whether to adopt it instead of running always-on services.

Rather than keeping services running at all times, {ServerlessProductName} scales workloads on-demand and to zero when idle, which can reduce resource consumption and management overhead. This approach tends to suit workloads with variable or unpredictable traffic, event-driven architectures, and short-lived functions triggered by specific events.
