= Control who can invoke your workloads and send them events
include::_attributes/common-attributes.adoc[]
:context: control-who-can-invoke-your-workloads

toc::[]

[role="_abstract"]
Restrict which clients can reach your Knative services and which sources can deliver events to your event consumers. Use JSON Web Token authentication for request-driven workloads, EventPolicy for event-driven workloads, and TLS or SASL credentials to authenticate your broker to Apache Kafka.
