= Integrating with email
:context: integrate-with-email

toc::[]

[role="_abstract"]
With {rh-rhacs-first}, you can configure your existing email provider to send notifications about policy violations.
If you use {rh-rhacscs-first}, you can use your existing email provider or the built-in email notifier to send email notifications.

You can use the `Default recipient` field to forward alerts from {product-title-short} and the {product-title-managed-short} to an email address.
Otherwise, you can use annotations to define an audience and tell them about policy violations associated with a specific deployment or namespace.
