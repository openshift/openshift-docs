= Troubleshoot component and workload issues

[role="_abstract"]
Cluster administrators and application teams need to diagnose and resolve problems with the components and workloads that run on their {product-title} cluster so that their applications stay healthy and available.

Troubleshooting components and workloads helps you:

* *Identify causes*: Distinguish workload failures from problems in supporting components.
* *Keep applications running*: Detect and resolve workload failures before they affect your users.
* *Restore application health*: Apply corrective actions within your control and recognize when a problem requires escalating to Red{nbsp}Hat.
