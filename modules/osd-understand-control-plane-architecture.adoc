= Control plane architecture and core services

[role="_abstract"]
The control plane is composed of control plane machines that manage your {product-title} cluster and the workloads that run on its compute machines.
Understanding how machine roles, Operators, and the etcd key-value store fit together helps you evaluate what Red{nbsp}Hat manages on your behalf and what remains under your control.
