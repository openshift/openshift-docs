= Verifying role-based access to secrets resources

[role="_abstract"]
To confirm that your permissions let you manage secrets resources without cluster administrator privileges, verify access in the {external-secrets-console-plugin}.

.Prerequisites

* You are logged in to the web console.
* You have access to a project with role-based access control (RBAC) permissions for the relevant custom resources (CRs), such as `Issuer` or `SecretStore`.

.Procedure

. Navigate to *Plugins* -> *Secrets Management*.

. Verify that the {external-secrets-console-plugin} detects and displays the installed secrets Operators.

. Open the project that contains your resources.

. Inspect the resource tables, such as the `Issuer` table.

. Confirm that the tables list your namespaced resources without a global error banner.

. Test the actions that are available for a resource:

.. Open the *Overflow* menu next to the resource.

.. Select an authorized action, such as *View* or *Edit*.

.. Confirm that the action completes without an error.

. Attempt an operation outside your permissions.

. Confirm that the notification banner identifies the missing RBAC permission.

.Verification

* Confirm that the {external-secrets-console-plugin} lists the installed Operators and does not display the `No Operators installed` warning.
