= Pod disruption budgets for virtualized control planes

[role="_abstract"]
A `PodDisruptionBudget` (PDB) prevents the hosting cluster from evicting more than one control plane VM at a time during node drain or maintenance operations.
You can configure a PDB to ensure that at least two control plane VMs remain running, which preserves `etcd` quorum.

Without a PDB, draining multiple hosting cluster nodes concurrently could evict more than one control plane VM at a time and cause quorum loss.
