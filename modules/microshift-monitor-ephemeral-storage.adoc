= Monitor ephemeral storage usage to prevent pod eviction

[role="_abstract"]
Monitor ephemeral storage usage on your {microshift-short} node to track disk space consumption on the `/var/lib/kubelet` and `/var/lib/containers` paths. Regular monitoring helps you identify storage-hungry workloads and adjust resource limits before the kubelet evicts pods because of storage exhaustion.
