= Remotely monitor the health of {product-title} clusters

[role="_abstract"]
Monitor cluster health and identify issues using Telemetry, the {insights-operator}, and Red{nbsp}Hat Lightspeed advisor service.

{product-title} collects telemetry and configuration data to provide insights about cluster health, identify potential issues, and deliver proactive recommendations. This remote health monitoring capability uses two main components:

* *Telemetry* - The Telemeter Client sends lightweight cluster metrics to Red{nbsp}Hat for subscription management, health monitoring, and support.
* *{insights-operator}* - Gathers detailed configuration data and sends it to Red{nbsp}Hat to identify potential issues and provide recommendations through the Red{nbsp}Hat Lightspeed advisor service.

A cluster that reports data to Red{nbsp}Hat through Telemetry and the {insights-operator} is considered a _connected cluster_.
