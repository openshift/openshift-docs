= Standards compliance for Telecom Grandmaster on Intel Granite Rapids-D

[role="_abstract"]
Telecom Grandmaster (T-GM) deployments on Intel Granite Rapids-D (GNR-D) platforms comply with specific ITU-T timing standards for accuracy, holdover, and traceability.
Understand the supported standards, known non-conformances, and general availability (GA) conformance requirements before deploying T-GM in production environments.

[id="nw-ptp-gnrd-standards-compliance-reference-prtc_{context}"]
== ITU-T G.8272 PRTC-B compliance

Telecom Grandmaster deployments on Intel Granite Rapids-D hardware comply with ITU-T G.8272 Primary Reference Time Clock (PRTC) Class-B timing accuracy requirements.

Timing accuracy:: ±40 nanoseconds relative to Coordinated Universal Time (UTC)
+
When the T-GM is locked to a GNSS time source, timing accuracy remains within ±40 nanoseconds of UTC, meeting ITU-T G.8272 PRTC-B classification requirements.

Traceability to GNSS time source:: The GNSS antenna connected to the NAC adapter provides the primary timing reference traceable to UTC.
+
The T-GM maintains traceability to UTC through continuous GNSS signal reception and DPLL synchronization.

PRTC-B classification requirements:: The T-GM operates as a PRTC-B clock when locked to a GNSS time source, providing the highest timing accuracy classification for telecom applications.

For more information about ITU-T G.8272 timing requirements, see link:https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.8272[ITU-T Recommendation G.8272].

[id="nw-ptp-gnrd-standards-compliance-reference-holdover_{context}"]
== ITU-T G.8273.2 Class-B holdover compliance

Telecom Grandmaster deployments on Intel Granite Rapids-D hardware comply with ITU-T G.8273.2 Class-B holdover performance requirements.

Holdover duration:: Minimum 4 hours
+
The Microchip DPLL maintains timing accuracy for a minimum of 4 hours during GNSS signal loss, meeting ITU-T G.8273.2 Class-B holdover duration requirements.

Time error during holdover:: Maximum 1.5 microseconds
+
Time error remains within ±1.5 microseconds during the holdover period, ensuring downstream devices continue receiving timing within ITU-T G.8273.2 Class-B specifications.

Holdover performance validation:: Validate holdover performance by disconnecting the GNSS antenna and monitoring timing offset metrics during the holdover period.
+
For more information about testing holdover behavior, see "Verifying Telecom Grandmaster DPLL synchronization on Intel Granite Rapids-D hardware".

For more information about ITU-T G.8273.2 holdover requirements, see link:https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.8273.2[ITU-T Recommendation G.8273.2].

[id="nw-ptp-gnrd-standards-compliance-reference-g8275_{context}"]
== ITU-T G.8275.1 profile conformance status

Telecom Grandmaster deployments on Intel Granite Rapids-D hardware implement the ITU-T G.8275.1 Precision Time Protocol (PTP) profile for telecom applications with known non-conformances.

Known non-conformances:: Validation testing with Paragon-neo conformance tools identified specific test cases that do not pass.
+
All Paragon-neo test failures are documented as formal non-conformances with product management approval.

SyncE support deferred:: Synchronous Ethernet (SyncE) general availability is deferred to a future release.
+
Full ITU-T G.8275.1 conformance requires SyncE support, which is not included in {product-title} {product-version}.

Partial standard compliance in {product-title} {product-version}:: The T-GM implements the ITU-T G.8275.1 PTP profile but does not claim full conformance due to known non-conformances and deferred SyncE support.

For more information about ITU-T G.8275.1 PTP profile requirements, see link:https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.8275.1[ITU-T Recommendation G.8275.1].

[id="nw-ptp-gnrd-standards-compliance-reference-paragon_{context}"]
== Paragon-neo validation test results

Paragon-neo is an ITU-T G.8275.1 conformance test suite used to validate Precision Time Protocol (PTP) implementations.
Validation testing on Intel Granite Rapids-D hardware identified specific test cases that do not pass.

Test cases passed:: The T-GM passes the majority of Paragon-neo test cases for ITU-T G.8275.1 conformance.

Test cases failed with waivers:: Specific test cases do not pass and are documented as formal non-conformances with product management sign-off.
+
Product management reviews each non-conformance and approves waivers for known limitations that do not impact telco deployment requirements.

Non-conformance documentation:: All Paragon-neo test failures are documented as formal non-conformances with detailed descriptions of the failure mode, impact analysis, and product management approval.

[id="nw-ptp-gnrd-standards-compliance-reference-ga_{context}"]
== General availability conformance criteria

The following conformance criteria must be met before Telecom Grandmaster on Intel Granite Rapids-D hardware moves from Technology Preview to general availability:

All Paragon-neo failures documented:: Every Paragon-neo test failure must be documented as a formal non-conformance with product management sign-off.

PM approval for waivers:: Product management must review and approve waivers for all known non-conformances.

SyncE general availability:: Synchronous Ethernet (SyncE) support must reach general availability to claim full ITU-T G.8275.1 conformance.
+
SyncE general availability is a prerequisite for moving the T-GM to general availability status.
