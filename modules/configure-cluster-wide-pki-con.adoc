= Configure a cluster-wide PKI

[role="_abstract"]
You can add your organization's custom Certificate Authority (CA) certificates to the cluster-wide truststore in {product-title}. Adding a custom PKI ensures secure communication between internal components and allows workloads to trust your internal or self-signed certificates.
