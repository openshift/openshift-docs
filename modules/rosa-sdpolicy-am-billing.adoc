= Billing and pricing

[role="_abstract"]
Understanding how {product-title} billing works helps you plan costs and choose the right commitment model for your organization. {product-title} is billed directly to your {AWS} account. {product-title} pricing is consumption based, with annual commitments or three-year commitments for greater discounting. The total cost of {product-title} consists of two components:

* {product-title} service fees
* AWS infrastructure fees

For more details, visit the "{product-title} Pricing" page on the AWS website, linked in the _Additional resources_.
