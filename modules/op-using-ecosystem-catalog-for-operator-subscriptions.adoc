= Using the Red Hat Ecosystem Catalog to browse Operators

[role="_abstract"]
If you do not have cluster-admin rights and cannot access the OperatorHub directly in {ocp}, you can use the Red Hat Ecosystem Catalog to browse available Operators, including {pipelines-title}.

The Red Hat Ecosystem Catalog is available at link:https://catalog.redhat.com[https://catalog.redhat.com]. You can filter the catalog to show only Operators that are available for {ocp} by selecting the following filters:

* **Deployed as**: Operator
* **Target platforms**: Red Hat OpenShift

== Understanding Operator subscription requirements

Some Operators in the Red Hat Ecosystem Catalog require additional subscriptions beyond your base {ocp} subscription. For example, certain Operators might require:

* A separate product subscription
* An add-on subscription
* A specific support tier

Currently, subscription requirement information is displayed only on the individual Operator detail pages. To check the subscription requirements for an Operator:

.Procedure
. Navigate to link:https://catalog.redhat.com/en/search?searchType=Software&deployed_as=Operator&target_platforms=Red+Hat+OpenShift[Red Hat Ecosystem Catalog - Operators for OpenShift].
. Click on the Operator you want to learn about.
. On the Operator detail page, look for the subscription requirements section to understand what subscriptions are needed.

[NOTE]
====
The {pipelines-title} Operator is included with your {ocp} subscription and does not require an additional subscription.
====

== Requesting Operator installation

After identifying an Operator that meets your requirements and verifying the subscription requirements, you can request that your cluster administrator install the Operator on your cluster.

Provide your administrator with:

* The name of the Operator
* The subscription requirements (if any)
* The namespace where you need the Operator functionality
