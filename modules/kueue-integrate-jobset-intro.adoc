= Integrate the {js-operator} with {kueue-name}

[role="_abstract"]
You can integrate the {js-operator} with {kueue-name} to apply {kueue-name} scheduling and resource management to large-scale, coordinated workloads such as high-performance computing (HPC) and AI training. The {js-operator} models a distributed batch workload as a group of Kubernetes jobs, so you can specify different pod templates for distinct groups of pods.
