= VM disaster recovery strategy

[role="_abstract"]
{VirtProductName} supports using disaster recovery (DR) solutions to ensure that your environment can recover after a site outage. To use these solutions, you must establish and validate a site-level disaster recovery strategy with defined recovery time objective (RTO) and recovery point objective (RPO) targets for virtual machine workloads when you plan your {VirtProductName} deployment.
