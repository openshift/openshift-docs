= Configure TLS for gateways

[role="_abstract"]

Secure traffic entering and leaving the mesh by configuring Transport Layer Security (TLS) on your gateways. Mutual TLS (mTLS) encrypts traffic between services inside the mesh but does not protect traffic at the mesh boundary. You must configure TLS on your gateways separately to encrypt ingress and egress connections.
