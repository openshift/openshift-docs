= Red{nbsp}Hat {ServerlessProductName} 1.37.2

[role="_abstract"]
{ServerlessProductName} 1.37.2 is now available. This release of {ServerlessProductName} addresses identified Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVEs) to enhance security and reliability. The following notes describe the fixed issues that affect {ServerlessProductName} on {ocp-product-title}.

[id="serverless-fixed-issues-1-37-2_{context}"]
== Fixed issues

Kafka consumer groups persisting after Trigger or Subscription deletion::

Before this update, two related issues caused Kafka consumer groups to persist after deleting a Trigger or Subscription. First, consumer group finalization ran before dispatchers stopped consuming. This resulted in `kafka server: The group is not empty` errors. After five retries, the system cleared the finalizer and abandoned the deletion. Second, under high load, race conditions in the dispatcher orphaned consumers. These consumers continued sending heartbeats to Kafka. As a consequence, affected consumer groups remained active indefinitely and `ConsumerGroup` custom resource finalization stalled.
+
With this release, consumer group deletion waits for dispatchers to stop consuming before proceeding. The system retries these errors without a bounded limit and removes stale egress entries from ConfigMaps on pod removal. The system also serializes consumer operations to prevent race conditions. As a result, deleting a Trigger or Subscription fully removes the associated Kafka consumer group.
+
