= Uninstalling the {gitops-shortname} Operator

[role="_abstract"]
After you delete the Argo CD instances, uninstall the {gitops-title} Operator from *Installed Operators*.

.Procedure

. In the *Administrator* perspective of the web console, click *Operators* -> *Installed Operators*.
. From the *Project* list, select the `openshift-gitops-operator` namespace.
. On the {gitops-title} Operator, open the options menu and click *Uninstall Operator*.
. Confirm the uninstall.
