= Troubleshoot hosted clusters by platform

[role="_abstract"]
When a hosted cluster does not come up or its nodes do not register, the cause is often specific to the platform the hosted cluster runs on. You start by gathering must-gather data from the management cluster, then work through the symptoms for your platform.
