= Plan your deployment

[role="_abstract"]
Assess infrastructure requirements, understand platform limits, and plan capacity before deploying {VirtProductName}, so you can size your environment correctly and avoid costly misconfigurations.

Understanding the planning considerations for {VirtProductName} helps you make informed decisions about cluster sizing, storage requirements, and platform compatibility. This ensures your deployment meets performance requirements and avoids resource constraints that could impact virtual machine workloads.
