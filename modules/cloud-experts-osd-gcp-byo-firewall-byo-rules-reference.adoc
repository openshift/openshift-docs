= Bring your own (BYO) firewall rules reference

[role="_abstract"]
The following table summarizes the eight bring your own (BYO) firewall rules that you create in xref:cloud-experts-osd-gcp-byo-firewall-creating-byo-rules_{context}[Creating bring your own (BYO) firewall rules].

.BYO firewall rules
[cols="1,3,2,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Number |Rule |Ports |Source |Target service account

|1
|`<byo_prefix>-api`
|`tcp:6443`
|`0.0.0.0/0`
|Control plane

|2
|`<byo_prefix>-etcd`
|`tcp:2379-2380`
|Control plane service account
|Control plane

|3
|`<byo_prefix>-health-checks`
|`tcp:6080,tcp:6443,tcp:22624`
|{GCP} load balancer CIDR ranges
|Control plane

|4
|`<byo_prefix>-control-plane`
|`tcp:2379-2380,tcp:6443,tcp:9258-9259,tcp:10257-10259,tcp:22623-22624`
|Control plane service account
|Control plane

|5
|`<byo_prefix>-internal-network`
|`tcp,udp` (various ports)
|Control plane and worker service accounts
|Control plane and worker

|6
|`<byo_prefix>-internal-cluster`
|`tcp,udp,icmp` (all ports)
|Control plane and worker service accounts
|Control plane and worker

|7
|`<byo_prefix>-ingress-k8s-fw`
|`tcp:80,tcp:443`
|`0.0.0.0/0`
|Worker

|8
|`<byo_prefix>-ingress-k8s-http-hc`
|`tcp:30000-32767`
|{GCP} load balancer CIDR ranges
|Worker
|===
