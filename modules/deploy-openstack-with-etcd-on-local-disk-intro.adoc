= Deploy OpenStack with etcd on local disk

[role="_abstract"]
After installation, you can resolve and prevent performance issues of your {rh-openstack-first} installation by moving etcd from a root volume (provided by {rh-openstack} Cinder) to a dedicated ephemeral local disk.
