= View Ingress Controller logs

[role="_abstract"]
You can view your Ingress Controller logs.

.Procedure

* View your Ingress Controller logs:
+
[source,terminal]
----
$ oc logs --namespace=openshift-ingress-operator deployments/ingress-operator -c <container_name>
----
