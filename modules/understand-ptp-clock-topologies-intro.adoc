= Understand PTP clock topologies

[role="_abstract"]
You can compare the PTP clock topologies that {product-title} supports, from single-NIC ordinary and boundary clocks to dual-port and GNSS-assisted grandmaster designs.
Choose a topology before you configure the linuxptp services.

For the configuration procedures, see the "Configure ordinary and boundary clocks" and "Configure grandmaster clocks" sections of the "Configure PTP clock synchronization" job in the Networking category.
