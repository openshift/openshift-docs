= Previewing and editing an attachment

[role="_abstract"]
Preview an attachment before you submit a question, and optionally edit its content. Editing an attachment is useful when you want to remove sensitive information or reduce the attachment to only the relevant content.

.Prerequisites

* You have attached context to your question.

.Procedure

. Click the {ols-official} icon in the lower-right corner of the screen.

. Click the attachment label below the message field.
+
The *Preview attachment* window opens and displays the attachment content.

. Optional: To change the content of an editable attachment, complete the following steps:

.. Click *Edit*.

.. Modify the content in the editor.

.. Click *Save*.
+
To discard your changes and restore the original content, click *Revert to original*.

. Click *Dismiss*.

. Enter a question.

. Click the *Submit* button.
+
{ols-long} returns information based on your question and the attachment.
