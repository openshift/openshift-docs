= Troubleshooting cluster notifications

[role="_abstract"]
If you are not receiving cluster notification emails, troubleshoot the issue by checking email filters, verifying notification contacts, and confirming network access.

.Procedure

. Ensure that emails sent from `@redhat.com` addresses are not filtered out of your email inbox.

. Ensure that your correct email address is listed as a notification contact for the cluster.

. If you are not listed as a notification contact, ask the cluster owner or administrator to add you as a notification contact.

. If your cluster does not receive notifications, ensure that your cluster can access resources at `api.openshift.com`.
