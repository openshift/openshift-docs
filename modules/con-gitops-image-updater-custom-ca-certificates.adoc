= Custom CA certificates for container registries

[role="_abstract"]
When Argo CD Image Updater connects to container registries that use certificates signed by a private Certificate Authority (CA), you need to configure the CA certificates so Image Updater can verify the TLS connection.

Image Updater supports three methods for providing custom CA certificates, listed in order of precedence:

. *File path configuration*: Mount your CA certificate bundle as a volume and reference it in the registry configuration.

. *Inline data configuration*: Embed the CA certificate directly in the registry configuration for smaller deployments or when you cannot mount volumes conveniently.

. *Automatic discovery*: Image Updater automatically discovers CA certificates from Argo CD's `argocd-tls-certs-cm` ConfigMap.
