== Fixed issues

* Before this update, the `kube-apiserver-to-kubelet` client signer certificate was being refreshed every 30 days despite having a 365-day validity period. With this release, the refresh interval is corrected to 292 days, 80% of validity, which is consistent with other signers. As a result, certificate rotations are prevented and the signer behavior is aligned with the intended configuration. (link:https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/OCPBUGS-128389[OCPBUGS-128389])
