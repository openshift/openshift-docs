= Monitor platform vulnerabilities and audit security incidents

[role="_abstract"]
Monitoring and auditing a cluster is an important part of safeguarding it against inappropriate usage. Cluster events, logging, and audit logs give you the record you need to detect known vulnerabilities, investigate suspicious activity, and show what changed on the cluster and who changed it.
