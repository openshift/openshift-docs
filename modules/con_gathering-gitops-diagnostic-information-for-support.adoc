= Escalate to Red Hat Support

[role="_abstract"]
Escalate issues to Red Hat Support by collecting diagnostic information about your cluster. You can use the `must-gather` tool to collect diagnostic information for project-level resources, cluster-level resources, and {gitops-title} components.

[NOTE]
====
For prompt support, provide diagnostic information for both {OCP} and {gitops-title}.
====
