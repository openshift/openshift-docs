= Understand disconnected environments

[role="_abstract"]
A disconnected environment is an environment that does not have full access to the internet. {product-title} is designed to perform many automatic functions that depend on an internet connection, such as retrieving release images from a registry or retrieving update paths and recommendations for the cluster. Without a direct internet connection, you must perform additional setup and configuration for your cluster to maintain full functionality in the disconnected environment.
