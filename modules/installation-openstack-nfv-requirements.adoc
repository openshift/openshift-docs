= Requirements for clusters on {rh-openstack} that use either SR-IOV or OVS-DPDK

[role="_abstract"]
If you use SR-IOV or OVS-DPDK with your deployment, you must meet certain requirements.

Ensure that the flavor for your {product-title} compute machines supports huge pages and that the underlying {rh-openstack} Compute nodes have huge pages configured.
