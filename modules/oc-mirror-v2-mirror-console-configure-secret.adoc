= Configure a pull secret

[role="_abstract"]
To authenticate with the registries that hold mirrored content, upload or paste a pull secret in the OpenShift Mirror Console, and then verify authentication. Mirror operations stay unavailable until you configure the pull secret and authentication succeeds.

.Prerequisites

* The OpenShift Mirror Console is open in a browser.
* You have a pull secret from the {hybrid-console}.

.Procedure

. Click *Settings* to open the console settings.

. Click the *Pull Secret* tab to add or update the pull secret used for registry access.
+
If you have not configured a pull secret, the console displays a `No pull secret detected` message.

. Provide your pull secret by doing one of the following:

.. Drag and drop your `pull-secret.json` file onto the upload area.

.. Click *Browse* and select the file.

.. Paste the pull secret content into the *Value* field.

. Click *Save* to store the pull secret in the OpenShift Mirror Console.

. Click the *Registry* tab to review the registry authentication status.
+
The *Registry Authentication* list shows registries with a *Not verified* status.

. Click *Verify All* to authenticate against the listed registries.

.Verification

* Confirm that each registry in the *Registry Authentication* list shows an *Authenticated* status.
