= Configure a pull secret

[role="_abstract"]
To authenticate with the registries that hold mirrored content, upload or paste a pull secret in the OpenShift Mirror Console, and then verify authentication. Configure the pull secret before starting mirror operations.

You can provide a pull secret by dragging a file, pasting its contents, or browsing for the file. Complete the step for your preferred method.

.Prerequisites

* You have opened the OpenShift Mirror Console in a browser.
* You have a pull secret from the {hybrid-console}.

.Procedure

. Click *Settings* to open the console settings.

. Click the *Pull Secret* tab to add or update the pull secret used for registry access.
+
If you have not configured a pull secret, the console displays a `No pull secret detected` message.

. To upload by dragging, drag and drop your `pull-secret.json` file onto the upload area.

. To paste a pull secret, enter its contents in the *Value* field.

. To upload by browsing, complete the following steps:

.. Click *Browse*.

.. Select the file.

. Click *Save* to store the pull secret in the OpenShift Mirror Console.

. Click the *Registry* tab to review the registry authentication status.
+
The *Registry Authentication* list shows registries with a *Not verified* status.

. Click *Verify All* to authenticate against the listed registries.

.Verification

* Confirm that each registry in the *Registry Authentication* list shows an *Authenticated* status.
