= Policy violations and security findings

[role="_abstract"]
The built-in policies in {product-title-short} identify a variety of security findings, including vulnerabilities (CVEs), violations of DevOps best practices, high-risk build and deployment practices, and suspicious runtime behaviors.

Whether you use the default out-of-box security policies or your own custom policies, {product-title-short} reports a violation when an enabled policy fails.
