= Deploy in ambient mode

[role="_abstract"]

Ambient mode uses a node-level proxy (ztunnel) to provide mTLS encryption and L4 traffic management without injecting sidecar containers into application pods. You can optionally deploy waypoint proxies to enable L7 traffic management and policy enforcement for specific namespaces or services.
