= AI model training and product improvement policies

[role="_abstract"]
{red-hat} does not use customer prompt or response data to train or fine-tune AI models.

When telemetry is enabled, {red-hat} uses the collected data exclusively to evaluate and refine internal system prompts and embedding parameters to improve {ols-long} Service quality. Any interaction data that is retained for internal evaluation is heavily redacted and anonymized before use.

{red-hat} does not train or tune its own AI models using customer interaction data, and does not share customer interaction data with third-party AI model providers for training purposes.
