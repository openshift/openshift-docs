= Manage jobs and workloads with {kueue-name}

[role="_abstract"]
When you create jobs in your cluster, {kueue-name} represents each job as a `Workload` object that tracks resource requirements, decisions, and statuses. {kueue-name} does not manipulate your jobs directly; it manages the `Workload` objects and syncs decisions and statuses between the two.
