= Configuring startup probing

[role="_abstract"]
For {ServerlessProductName} Serving, startup probes are not defined by default. You can define startup probes for your containers in your deployment configuration. 

.Procedure

* Define startup probes for your service by modifying your deployment configuration. The following example shows a configuration with two containers:
+
.Example of defined startup probes
[source,yaml]
----
apiVersion: serving.knative.dev/v1
kind: Service
# ...
spec:
  template:
    spec:
       containers:
        - name: first-container
          image: <image>
          ports:
            - containerPort: 8080
          # ...
          startupProbe:
          httpGet:
            port: 8080
            path: "/"
        - name: second-container
          image: <image>
          # ...
          startupProbe:
          httpGet:
            port: 8081
            path: "/"
----
+
--
where:

`startupProbe` (first-container)::
The startup probe for `first-container`.

`startupProbe` (second-container)::
The startup probe for `second-container`.
