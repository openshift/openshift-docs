= Opting out of autopilot management by using the web console

[role="_abstract"]
You can use the {product-title} web console to opt out of `virt-platform-autopilot` controller management for a specific capability. When you opt out, the controller stops reconciling the configuration for that capability, and you take full ownership of its configuration.

.Prerequisites

* You have access to the cluster as a user with `cluster-admin` privileges.
* The `virt-platform-autopilot` controller is enabled on the cluster.
* The operator that you want to opt out of autopilot management is installed on the cluster.

.Procedure

. In the web console, navigate to *Virtualization* -> *Settings* -> *Recommended Capabilities*.

. In the *Autopilot-managed capabilities* table, locate the capability that you want to opt out of.

. Click the options menu {kebab} for the capability, and select *Review recommendation*.

. In the *Review recommendation* modal, opt out of autopilot management for the capability.
+
The *Configuration status* for the capability changes to *Manual*.

.Verification

* In the *Autopilot-managed capabilities* table, verify that the *Configuration status* column shows *Manual* for the capability.
