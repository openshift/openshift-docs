= Manage {op-system-base} repositories for RPM updates

[role="_abstract"]
When you apply manual RPM updates, carefully manage your {op-system-base-full} repositories to avoid creating an unsupported configuration or breaking your node. Keeping {microshift-short} and {op-system-base} in a supported configuration helps ensure reliable updates.
