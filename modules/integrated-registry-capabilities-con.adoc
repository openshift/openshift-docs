= Integrated registry capabilities

[role="_abstract"]
{product-title} provides an internal, integrated container image registry that can build images from your source code, deploy them, and manage their lifecycle. Reviewing what the integrated registry supports, including its authentication behavior and how it relates to third-party registries, helps you decide how to use it.
