= Integrate the {lws-operator} with {kueue-name}

[role="_abstract"]
You can integrate the {lws-operator} with {kueue-name} to apply {kueue-name} scheduling and resource management when running LeaderWorkerSets. The {lws-operator} manages multi-node AI/ML inference deployments by deploying a group of pods as a single unit of replication.
