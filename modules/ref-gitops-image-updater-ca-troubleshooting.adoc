= Troubleshoot CA certificate configuration

[role="_abstract"]
If Image Updater cannot connect to your registry after you configure CA certificates, follow these steps.

. Check that the certificate is in valid PEM format.

. Ensure the certificate is not expired.

. Review Image Updater logs for certificate validation errors.
