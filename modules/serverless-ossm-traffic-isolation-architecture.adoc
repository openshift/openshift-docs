= High-level architecture

[role="_abstract"]
The {SMProductShortName} sidecars injected into your pods enforce `AuthorizationPolicy` rules that isolate network traffic between tenants.

The high-level architecture of {ServerlessProductShortName} traffic isolation provided by {SMProductShortName} consists of `AuthorizationPolicy` objects in the `knative-serving`, `knative-eventing`, and the tenants' namespaces, with all the components being part of the {SMProductShortName}.
