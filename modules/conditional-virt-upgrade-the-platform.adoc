= Upgrade the platform

[role="_abstract"]
Keep {VirtProductName} updated and compatible with {product-title}. Staying on a supported update path keeps your environment fully supported and avoids disruption to VM workloads.

You can choose how much control you want over the update process, from fully automatic updates to manually reviewing and approving each change. You can also opt in to early access builds to try new capabilities before they reach general availability.

Review the following topics to configure your update settings, understand what to expect during an update, and keep Extended Update Support (EUS) upgrades from disrupting VM workloads. They also cover {op-system-base} 9 machine-type compatibility for VM templates after an update.
