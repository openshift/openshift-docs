= Verifying scanner installation

[role="_abstract"]
After installation, use the portal to verify that the scanner works correctly.

.Procedure

. In the {product-title-short} portal, go to *Platform Configuration -> Clusters*.
. In the *Clusters* view, select a cluster to view its details.
. In the *Health Status* card, verify that *Scanner* appears and shows *Healthy*.
