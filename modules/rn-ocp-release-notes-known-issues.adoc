= Known issues

[role="_abstract"]
You can review the list of known issues in {product-title} 5.0 to see if any issues affect your clusters or environments.

* When you use the {op-system-base-full} 10 stream, which is the default for new installs of 5.0 but not for upgrades from 4.22, throughput of messages less than 4KB in size is approximately 30-50% lower than {op-system-base} 9. The regression is most pronounced at small message sizes, for example, 64 bytes and 1 KB, and diminishes at message sizes of 4 KB or larger.
