= Consequences of disabling Telemetry

[role="_abstract"]
In {rh-rhacs-first} version 4.0, you can opt out of Telemetry.
However, the product embeds telemetry as a core component, so opting out is strongly discouraged.
Opting out of telemetry limits the ability of Red{nbsp}Hat to understand how everyone uses the product and to rank areas for improvements.
