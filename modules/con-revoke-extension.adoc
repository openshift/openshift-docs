= Revoke an extension

[role="_abstract"]
Revoke an extension's permission to register its policy `Kind` by deleting the `ClusterRoleBinding` for the extension's `ServiceAccount`. You do not need to manage `Secrets`.

Deleting the binding does not stop the extension workload itself. To stop it completely, also delete or scale down its `Deployment`.

Revocation takes effect at the extension's next handshake. A session that is already established continues to work until the connection drops, the {prodname} Operator restarts, or the extension stops making calls for longer than the session timeout.

At the next extension handshake attempt, the {prodname} Operator rejects the extension. The extension can no longer query the topology, publish data bindings, or commit pipeline actions.
