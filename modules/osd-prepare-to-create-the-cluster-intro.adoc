= Prepare to create the cluster

[role="_abstract"]
As a cluster administrator, prepare your environment before you create an {product-title} cluster on Amazon Web Services (AWS). When you review the requirements and configure your AWS account in advance, your account and network are ready for the cluster creation workflow.

Preparing before you create a cluster helps you to do the following:

* Confirm that your AWS account meets the customer, resource, and service requirements.
* Configure your AWS account and firewall for the cluster.
* Understand the AWS resources that {product-title} provisions on your behalf.
