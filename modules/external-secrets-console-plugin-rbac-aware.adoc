= Role-based access to secrets resources

[role="_abstract"]
You can use the {external-secrets-console-plugin} to view and manage the resources that your role-based access control (RBAC) permissions allow. You do not need cluster administrator privileges to work with secrets resources in your authorized namespaces.

Your RBAC permissions let you view the resources in your namespaces, even without `GET` access to the `customresourcedefinitions` resource. The {external-secrets-console-plugin} uses lists of those resources and API group discovery to detect installed Operators.

The plugin does not show a false `No Operators installed` warning when you have access to custom resources (CRs) in your namespaces.
