= Role-based access to secrets resources

[role="_abstract"]
Manage secrets resources in your authorized namespaces without cluster administrator privileges. The {external-secrets-console-plugin} shows the resources that your role-based access control (RBAC) permissions allow.

Your RBAC permissions let you view the resources in your namespaces, even without `GET` access to the `customresourcedefinitions` resource. The {external-secrets-console-plugin} uses lists of those resources and API group discovery to detect installed Operators.

The {external-secrets-console-plugin} does not show a false `No Operators installed` warning when you can access custom resources (CRs) in your namespaces.
