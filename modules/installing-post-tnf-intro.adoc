= Post-installation troubleshooting and recovery

[role="_abstract"]
You can troubleshoot and restore a two-node {product-title} cluster with fencing (TNF) after a disruption event. Manually recover services when automated recovery is unavailable, replace degraded control plane nodes, and use the `fencing_validator` script to verify cluster health.
