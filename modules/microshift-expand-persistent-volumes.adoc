= Expand persistent volumes

[role="_abstract"]
You can expand persistent volumes on {microshift-short} to increase storage capacity for running workloads without interrupting access to the data. {microshift-short} supports expansion of CSI volumes, local volumes, and filesystem-backed PVCs through LVMS.
