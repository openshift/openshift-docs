= Configure post-quantum cryptography (PQC)

[role="_abstract"]

Protect your mesh against future quantum computing threats by enabling {pqc} ({pqc-short}) for mesh-internal traffic, gateway traffic, or both. Each area is configured independently, so you can adopt {pqc-short} incrementally based on your security requirements.
