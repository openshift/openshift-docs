= Design enterprise baselines and recommended patterns

[role="_abstract"]
Cluster-wide network policy is easiest to maintain when it follows a consistent design. Recommended practices for `AdminNetworkPolicy` cover how to assign priorities, choose actions, and write selectors that do not accidentally capture system namespaces.
