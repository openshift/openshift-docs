= Bare metal, Nutanix, and vSphere considerations

[role="_abstract"]
Bare-metal, Nutanix, and vSphere installations do not automatically provision storage for the integrated image registry, so you must configure a backend yourself after installation. Knowing what each of these platforms expects helps you choose a supportable option.
