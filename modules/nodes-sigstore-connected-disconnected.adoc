= Connected and disconnected environments

[role="_abstract"]
Signature verification works differently depending on your environment. In connected environments, verification is transparent, while disconnected environments require mirroring signatures along with images.

Connected environments:: In connected environments, the cluster can directly access public registries such as `quay.io` and retrieve images and their associated sigstore signatures. The verification process is transparent and requires minimal configuration beyond creating the appropriate `ClusterImagePolicy` or `ImagePolicy` objects.

Disconnected environments:: In disconnected or air-gapped environments, you must mirror both the container images and their sigstore signatures to your local registry before the cluster can verify them. Use the oc-mirror plugin to mirror images with their signatures. If signatures are not mirrored, signature verification fails and pods cannot start.
