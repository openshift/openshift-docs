= Check the status of a cluster extension

[role="_abstract"]
When an extension does not install or update as expected, you can inspect its state to decide what to do next. {olmv1-first} reports progress through the rollout of the extension and surfaces the most common failure classes, such as unresolved catalog content, insufficient RBAC permissions, and invalid deployment configuration.
