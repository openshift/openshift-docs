= Protect services from failures and overload

[role="_abstract"]

After you deploy the mesh, configure it to maintain service availability and manage risk when rolling out application changes.

High availability for the control plane prevents single points of failure by running multiple `istiod` instances. You can set a static replica count or enable autoscaling based on resource usage.

Traffic routing lets you shift a percentage of traffic between application versions, so you can test new releases with a subset of users before a full rollout. In sidecar mode, you configure traffic splits with `VirtualService` and `DestinationRule` resources. In ambient mode, you use `HTTPRoute` resources routed through waypoint proxies.
