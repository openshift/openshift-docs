= Traffic health thresholds

[role="_abstract"]

The console marks request traffic degraded or failing by comparing the share of matching responses with thresholds in the `Kiali` custom resource. Pod status is a separate indicator and is not configurable. The health of a resource is the worst status of its indicators.

{KialiServer} uses the first `health_config.rate` entry whose namespace, kind, and name match the resource. Later entries are not merged into that match. Put specific namespaces above the mesh-wide default.

The default configuration treats HTTP 5xx responses more strictly than HTTP 4xx responses. gRPC status codes 1 through 16 are errors. Code `0` is success.

[source,yaml]
----
spec:
  health_config:
    rate:
    - namespace: ".*"
      kind: ".*"
      name: ".*"
      tolerance:
      - code: "^5\\d\\d$"
        direction: ".*"
        protocol: "http"
        degraded: 0
        failure: 10
      - code: "^4\\d\\d$"
        direction: ".*"
        protocol: "http"
        degraded: 10
        failure: 20
      - code: "^[1-9]$|^1[0-6]$"
        direction: ".*"
        protocol: "grpc"
        degraded: 0
        failure: 10
----

.Rate options
[cols="1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
|Field
|Definition
|Default

|`namespace`
|Namespaces to match, as a regular expression
|`.*`

|`kind`
|Resource types to match: `workload`, `app`, or `service`
|`.*`

|`name`
|Resource names to match, as a regular expression
|`.*`

|`tolerance`
|Tolerances to apply
|
|===

.Tolerance options
[cols="1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
|Field
|Definition
|Default

|`code`
|Response status codes to match, as a regular expression. Use `-` for requests that received no response.
|Required

|`direction`
|`inbound` or `outbound`
|`.*`

|`protocol`
|`http` or `grpc`
|`.*`

|`degraded`
|Degraded when the matching share of requests is at least this percentage
|`0`

|`failure`
|Failure when the matching share of requests is at least this percentage
|`0`
|===

A rate at or above `failure` is a failure. A rate at or above `degraded` and below `failure` is degraded. A lower rate is healthy. No traffic is reported as no health information.

Override a code that is expected, such as HTTP 404, by adding a `rate` entry for that namespace before the default:

[source,yaml]
----
spec:
  health_config:
    rate:
    - namespace: "alpha"
      tolerance:
      - code: "404"
        protocol: "http"
        failure: 10
----
