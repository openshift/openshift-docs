= View and manage Rollouts in the GitOps Console

You can view, create, and manage Argo Rollouts through the {gitops-shortname} Console Plugin.

.Prerequisites

* The Rollout custom resource definition is installed on your cluster.
* You have access to the {OCP} web console.
* You have enabled the {gitops-shortname} Console plugin.
* Your permissions allow you to list Rollouts in one or more namespaces.

.Procedure

. Navigate to *GitOps* -> *Rollouts* in the *Administrator* perspective.

. Optional: Select a specific project from the *Project* dropdown to view Rollouts in that namespace, or select all projects to view Rollouts across all namespaces.

. Optional: Filter Rollouts by rollout status:
+
* *Rollout Status*: Healthy, Paused, Progressing, Degraded

. Optional: Click *Topology view* to open the Developer Topology graph showing Rollout workloads.

. To create a new Rollout:
+
.. Click *Create Rollout*.
.. Edit the YAML template with your rollout configuration including strategy (Blue-Green or Canary), replicas, and pod template.
.. Click *Create*.

. To view Rollout details, click a Rollout name in the list.
+
The details page provides the following tabs:
+
* *Details*: View and edit identity, scale, strategy, and status
* *YAML*: Edit the Rollout manifest
* *Revisions*: View revisions and perform rollout actions (Promote, Full Promote, Abort, Retry, Restart)
* *Pods*: View pods managed by the Rollout
* *Events*: View Kubernetes events

. To scale a Rollout, click the *Details* tab and use the inline scale controls to adjust the replica count.

. To manage rollout progression, click the *Revisions* tab and use the header actions menu to promote, abort, retry, or restart the rollout.
