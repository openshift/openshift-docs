= Health

[role="_abstract"]

Health in the console covers the mesh infrastructure and the applications running in the mesh. The status of a resource is the most severe status of its indicators.

== Infrastructure health

The masthead reports control-plane and observability problems, including multi-cluster problems. The color is the severity. Hover over the indicator to read the detail. The Mesh page shows the same components in a topology. Infrastructure status is described in "Mesh infrastructure status".

== Overview health

The overview groups applications by health and shows the services with the highest error rates and the highest 95th-percentile latency.

== Graph health

Nodes and edges use orange for degraded traffic and red for failing traffic. The edge color is the health of the requests between the two ends. The shape of a node is its type, not its health. The graph refreshes on the interval you set. You can pause it or replay an earlier interval.

== Health indicators

The console has four health states:

* No health information
* Healthy
* Degraded
* Failure
