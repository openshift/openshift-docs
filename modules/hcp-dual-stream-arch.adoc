= Supported architectures for {op-system-base} 10 NodePool resources

[role="_abstract"]
{op-system-base-full} 10 boot images for {hcp} `NodePool` resources are available for a subset of architectures and platform combinations.

Before pinning a `NodePool` resource to `spec.osImageStream.name: rhel-10`, verify that {op-system-base} 10 is available for your target architecture and platform. `NodePool` resources on architectures or platforms not listed in the {op-system-base} 10 column must use `spec.osImageStream.name: rhel-9`.

.{op-system-base} 10 platform support by architecture
[cols="2,1,6", options="header"]
|===
|Architecture
|{op-system-base} 10 supported
|Supported platforms for {op-system-base} 10

|x86_64
|Yes
|{aws-short}, {azure-short}, {gcp-first}, bare metal, {rh-openstack-first}, KubeVirt

|aarch64 (ARM64)
|Yes (limited)
|{aws-short}, {azure-short}, {gcp-first}, bare metal, {rh-openstack}

|ppc64le
|Yes (limited)
|Bare metal (Metal), {rh-openstack}, {ibm-power-server-name}

|s390x
|Yes (limited)
|{ibm-cloud-name}, KubeVirt, bare metal, {rh-openstack}

|===

[NOTE]
====
{op-system-base} 9 supports a broader set of platform and architecture combinations than {op-system-base} 10 in this release. For any platform or architecture where {op-system-base} 10 is not listed as supported, set `spec.osImageStream.name: rhel-9` on the `NodePool` resource.
====
