= Customize table columns
[role="_abstract"]

You can resize table columns to adjust the width and focus on the information you need.

.Prerequisites

* You have access to the {OCP} web console.
* The {gitops-shortname} Console plugin is enabled.

.Procedure

. In the {OCP} web console, navigate to *GitOps* and select a resource type.

. On the list page, position your cursor between two column headers until the resize handle appears.

. Click and drag the resize handle to adjust the column width to your preferred size.
+
The console stores your column width preferences in {OCP} console user settings. Your column widths follow you across different browsers and machines when you are logged in.

. Optional: To restore all column widths to their default sizes, click the *Reset column widths* option that appears after you resize columns.
