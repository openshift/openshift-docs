= Attaching container logs to your question

[role="_abstract"]
Attach the most recent lines from a container log to give {ols-long} log data for detailed troubleshooting and analysis.

.Procedure

. Navigate to a supported resource in the {ocp-product-title} web console. For example, click *Workloads* -> *Pods*, and then click the name of a pod.

. Click the {ols-official} icon in the lower-right corner of the screen.

. Click the attachment menu button (*+*) next to the message field, and then select *Logs*.
+
The *Configure log attachment* window opens.

. Optional: If the resource manages more than one pod, select a pod from the *Pod* list.

. Select a container from the *Container* list.

. Use the *Most recent lines* slider to set the number of log lines to attach. You can attach between 1 and 100 lines. The default is 25 lines.
+
A preview of the selected log lines is displayed.

. Click *Attach*.

. Enter a question.

. Click the *Submit* button.
+
{ols-long} returns information based on your question and the attached log.
