= Use interruptible capacity for lower-cost machines

[role="_abstract"]
A cloud provider sells its unused capacity at a lower price than its standard capacity, and can reclaim that capacity at short notice. A workload that tolerates interruption, such as a batch job or a stateless service that scales horizontally, can run on these machines instead.
