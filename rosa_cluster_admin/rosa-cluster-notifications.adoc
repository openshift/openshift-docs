[role="_abstract"]
As a cluster administrator, you need to stay informed about the health and status of your clusters so you can take action when issues arise or maintenance is required.
{product-title} automatically sends email notifications to keep you and your team updated on cluster events, incidents, upgrades, and maintenance activities.
You can configure who receives these alerts and understand what each notification means to ensure the right people respond at the right time.
