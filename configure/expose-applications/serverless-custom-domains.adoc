= Serve your application on your own domain
:context: serverless-custom-domains

[role="_abstract"]
Knative services are automatically assigned a default domain name based on your cluster configuration. You can serve your application on a domain that you own by mapping a custom domain name to the service with a `DomainMapping` resource.
